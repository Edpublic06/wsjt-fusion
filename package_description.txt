WSJT-Fusion is an independent amateur-radio software fork maintained by F6FDY, derived from WSJT-Z 2.0.19 by SQ9FVE and WSJT-X by the WSJT Development Group, with FT2 contributions from WS/WSJT-X Improved.
Version 1.0.0-beta1. The WSJT-X 3.3.0-beta1 engine migration and JTTY integration remain in progress.
See NOTICE.md and UPSTREAM-SOURCES.md for attribution, licenses and exact source provenance.
